Drawer rigid box

How a drawer rigid box is made

  1. Tray erected and corner-stayed

    The drawer starts as a greyboard blank, V-grooved, folded up and taped at the corners.

  2. Tray wrapped, lined and ribboned

    The tray is wrapped and turned in, lined inside, and a ribbon pull is fixed to its front wall.

  3. Sleeve laid out flat

    Four boards go down on one wrap: floor, wall, roof, wall, with a hinge gap between each. The edges are turned in and the inside is lined.

  4. Sleeve folded into a tube

    Wall up, roof over, wall down. The four boards fold round along their hinge gaps into a rigid sleeve.

  5. Drawer slides home

    The tray slides into the sleeve with just enough clearance to move freely, and draws back out on its ribbon.
