Lid & base rigid box

How a lid and base rigid box is made

  1. Two blanks, cut and V-grooved

    One blank for the base, one for the lid. The lid is cut a few millimetres larger all round, so it will slide over the finished base.

  2. Trays erected and corner-stayed

    The walls of both blanks fold up along the grooves and every corner is taped, locking each tray square.

  3. Wrapped and turned in

    Each tray is spotted onto its glued wrap. The paper is drawn up the walls and turned in over the top edge.

  4. Lined

    Lining paper covers the inside of both trays, hiding the board and the turned-in edges.

  5. Lid over base

    The lid is turned over and lowered onto the base. The fit is close enough that it settles slowly on a cushion of air.
