Shoulder-neck rigid box

How a shoulder-neck rigid box is made

  1. Three blanks, cut and V-grooved

    A base, a lid and a neck. The neck blank is slightly smaller in footprint and taller in the wall than the base it will sit inside.

  2. Trays erected and corner-stayed

    All three blanks fold up along their grooves and are taped at every corner.

  3. Wrapped and turned in

    Base and lid take the outer paper. The neck is wrapped in a contrast colour, because it is what shows when the lid lifts.

  4. Neck glued into the base

    The neck drops inside the base and is glued down. It stands proud of the rim, leaving a ledge all round: the shoulder.

  5. Lid seats on the shoulder

    The lid slides down over the neck and stops on the shoulder. Lid and base meet wall to wall, perfectly flush.
