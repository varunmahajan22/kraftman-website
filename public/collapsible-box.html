Collapsible rigid box

How a collapsible rigid box is made

  1. Boards on one wrap, with hinge gaps

    Base, four walls, lid, flap and four corner ears are all placed on a single wrap, with a gap between each board. Those gaps are what let the finished box fold flat.

  2. Magnets in, edges turned in

    Magnets are seated in the flap and the front wall, then the wrap is turned in over every exposed edge.

  3. Lined and taped

    Lining paper hides the boards and magnets. Peel-off adhesive strips go on the front and back walls, ready to hold the corners.

  4. Ships flat

    Finished boxes stack flat, a fraction of the height of an erected box, which cuts freight and warehouse space.

  5. Pops up in seconds

    Fold the ears in, lift the walls, press the corners onto the strips, and let the magnets close the lid.
